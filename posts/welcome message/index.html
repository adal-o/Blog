Welcome to Angel Outsider

by Amelie Wu & Raine Torres

An introduction of sorts and a little bit about us...

Welcome to Angel Outsider and thanks for dropping by :)

You may be wondering what this is and who we are, or maybe you already know us. To put it simply, we are two best friends who were proudly born and raised in the Valley. We very dearly love Los Angeles and hold it close to our hearts as the gift that keeps on giving.

Angel Outsider is many things, but at the forefront, it is our blog and a shared space to store our thoughts, reflections, hone in on our own writing practices, and much much more. Our wish is to have you, as our reader, engage with these thoughts and receive them with warm hands and minds. We are honored to have you join us in this exciting journey as we emerge and grow.
